## 🔬 Current Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of **Earth observation and machine learning**, with particular interest in:

- 🛰️ Satellite remote sensing of the atmosphere and hydrologic systems
- ❄️ Snowfall and precipitation retrieval from satellite observations
- 🧠 Deep learning for environmental and geophysical applications
- 🗺️ Multi-source geospatial data integration
- 🔄 Spatial gap filling, data reconstruction, and inpainting
- 🌦️ Climate monitoring, forecasting, and environmental prediction
- 📡 Radar and microwave remote sensing
- 🧪 Scientific machine learning and physics-informed data analysis
